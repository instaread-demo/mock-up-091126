ASUS Vivobook 2026 Lineup Gets New Colours: Cool Silver, Light Blue And Matte Gray Launching September 16

By Manoj Jain Sep 8, 2026 Asus

ASUS is preparing to refresh its Vivobook laptop range in India with a stronger focus on design and colours. The new ASUS Vivobook 2026 lineup will launch on September 16 under the campaign “Your Vibe. Your Colour.”

This time, ASUS is not making processor upgrades the only attraction. The company is introducing three fresh finishes: Cool Silver, Light Blue and Matte Gray.

The exact ASUS Vivobook 2026 price in India is still under wraps, but ASUS has already confirmed several important details about the new range.

What Is Different In The ASUS Vivobook 2026 Series

ASUS wants the latest Vivobook models to feel more personal instead of looking like regular office laptops.

The company is offering three colour choices so buyers can select a laptop based on their preferred style. Cool Silver and Matte Gray have a more classic look, while Light Blue adds a brighter and more youthful option.

The laptops will also feature a premium all-metal body, giving them a more upmarket feel.

Another major highlight is battery life. ASUS claims the new Vivobook models can deliver up to 38 hours of battery backup.

Of course, this number will depend on usage and will likely be achieved under ideal testing conditions. Still, the large battery-life claim shows that ASUS is focusing on students, professionals and travellers who may spend long hours away from a charger.

Five Vivobook Models Are Getting The Refresh

The new design and colour options will be available across five models.

These include the Vivobook 14 Flip, Vivobook S14 Flip, Vivobook S14, Vivobook S16 and Vivobook 16.

The Flip models use a 2-in-1 convertible design, which means the screen can fold around and the laptop can also be used in a tablet-like mode.

The regular S14, S16 and Vivobook 16 models follow the traditional clamshell laptop design.

ASUS is also expected to offer the lineup with AMD, Intel and Snapdragon processors.

This should give buyers more flexibility. Someone looking for a traditional Intel or AMD laptop will have those options, while buyers interested in Snapdragon-powered Windows laptops should also have models to consider.

Special Launch Offer At Rs 99

ASUS is also introducing a useful protection offer for buyers purchasing the new Vivobook models during the launch period.

Customers will be able to get 2 years of extended warranty and 3 years of accidental damage protection for just ₹99.

ASUS says this package is normally worth ₹5,599.

Accidental damage protection can be particularly useful for students and people who regularly carry their laptop in a backpack or travel with it.

ASUS Vivobook 2026 India Launch Date And Price

The ASUS Vivobook 2026 lineup will launch in India on September 16, 2026, at 12 Noon.

ASUS has not yet announced the exact price of every model and configuration.

Since the series includes different screen sizes, designs and processor options, pricing is expected to vary considerably between models.

Entry-level AMD and Intel versions could be priced lower, while higher-end configurations with more RAM, premium displays or Snapdragon processors may cost more.

If you want to check the current Vivobook models and their prices before the new lineup arrives, you can browse them here on Amazon.

You can also explore more ASUS Vivobook options through this Vivobook Amazon listing.

ASUS Is Betting More On Design This Time

Laptop specifications are becoming increasingly similar across brands, especially in popular price ranges.

Many models now offer comparable processors, memory options and displays. Design and colour can therefore become another important reason for buyers to choose one laptop over another.

With “Your Vibe. Your Colour.”, ASUS is clearly trying to make the Vivobook range more attractive to students and younger professionals who also care about how their laptop looks.

The three new finishes should help the laptops stand out from the usual black and dark-grey machines commonly seen in this segment.

The bigger question will be pricing.

Once ASUS reveals the official prices on September 16, it should become much easier to judge how competitive these new Vivobook models are against other laptops in the same segment.
