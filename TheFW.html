11 Big Chain Restaurants That Have Closed Locations in 2026

Some restaurant chains will end 2026 with more locations than they had at the start of the year.

As with most years, 2026 has seen its share of chain restaurants closing locations. Only so many can survive, after all.

READ MORE: How to Find the Only Remaining Rainforest Cafe Restaurants

But there is one type of chain restaurant that seems to come up more often in conversations about struggling chains: Pizza.

A quick search turned up at least four pizza chains that have closed a number of locations in 2026. And there are some big names included on that list, too.

Legacy pizza places like Pizza Hut and Papa John's are among the chains reducing locations. Both chains could potentially close as many as 200 locations each.

A Business Insider report noted that Papa John's may close an additional 100 restaurants in 2027.

From pizza joints to steakhouses, here are the chain restaurants saying goodbye to locations in 2026.

Keep scrolling to see the entire list.

Chain Restaurants That Are Closing or Have Closed Locations in 2026

The list of chains that have closed locations or have plans to shutter stores later in 2026 continues to grow at a rapid pace.

Gallery Credit: Rob Carroll

Getty Images

Bahama Breeze

The island-inspired Bahama Breeze is no more. According to Food Republic, the chain closed 14 of its 30 restaurants in April. The remaining locations will be rebranded as a different chain restaurant.

Getty Images

Denny's

Denny's closed several locations in 2025. But that might not have been the end of the restaurant slowly paring down locations.

Business Insider reports Denny's has closed additional locations in Michigan and Texas since the start of 2026.

Getty Images

Jack In The Box

Jack in the Box announced plans to close 150-200 underperforming stores in 2025, with additional stores planned to close in 2026. The company also revealed that it will spend less on new construction.

Getty Images

Noodles & Company

According to Business Insider, Noodles & Company is on a mission to "improve profitability and strengthen its overall performance" in 2026. The chain is reportedly looking to shutter 30-35 locations this year.

Getty Images

Outback Steakhouse

According to Food Republic, Outback Steakhouse started closing some of its locations in 2025, with plans to continue well into 2026. As many as 50 restaurants could close by the end of 2026.

Getty Images

Papa John's

Business Insider reports Papa John's will close as many as 200 stores in 2026. Unfortunately, the closings won't stop there. The chain will potentially shutter another 100 locations in 2027.

Getty Images

Papa Murphy's

Take-and-bake pizza chain Papa Murphy's announced in July that it would close as many as 50 locations. According to Fox Business, the closings were planned to be spread out as part of a 6-9 month process.

Getty Images

Pizza Hut

As many as 250 Pizza Hut locations could potentially close in 2026. ABC News reports the brand's parent company is considering selling the Pizza Hut chain as the store closings continue.

Getty Images

Red Robin

Red Robin is planning to close as many as 70 locations. It all started with 10-15 underperforming restaurants shutting down for good in 2025 with more closing in 2026.

Getty Images

Saks Off 5th and Last Call

Saks Global filed for bankruptcy in January. Following that move, the company will shutter nearly 60 Off 5th locations and another five stores branded with their Last Call concept.

Getty Images

Starbucks

The Street reported in January that Starbucks was set to "strategically close" hundreds of underperforming stores in 2026.

refrina // Shutterstock

Wendy's

Wendy's started closing underperforming locations in late 2025. The Street reports that as many as 350 of the chain's restaurants could be gone before the end of 2026.

Retail And Grocery Chain Stores That Have Closed Locations in 2026

The list of retail chain stores that have closed locations or have plans to shutter stores later in 2026 continues to grow at a rapid pace.

Gallery Credit: Rob Carroll

Getty Images

Allbirds

Allbirds announced at the beginning of 2026 that it would close its remaining full-price stores around the U.S. According to Business Insider, the shoe brand made the move to focus more on its e-commerce business.

Getty Images

Amazon Fresh and Amazon Go

Amazon plans to layoff thousands of workers as it begins to close all Amazon Fresh and Amazon Go stores, according to Marketplace.org.

Getty Images

Apple Store

Apple closed three of its stores in 2026. According to Business Insider, all three had one thing in common: They were all located in dying malls throughout the U.S.

Getty Images

Big 5 Sporting Goods

Big 5 Sporting Goods has been selling sports equipment and shoes for nearly 70 years. But that time might be nearing an end.

The Street reported in January that the chain would start closing stores in the coming months. Locations in Colorado and Idaho were set to be among the first to cease operations.

Getty Images

Big Lots

Big Lots was acquired out of bankruptcy court at the last minute at the start of 2025. Several locations closed throughout the year with more closings planned for 2026.

But there is a chance some stores may survive after all. Business of Home reports some locations will continue to carry Big Lots branding while others will be retooled to be "less furniture-centric" and possibly go by a different name.

Getty Images

Carter's

Children's clothing store Carter's announced in October 2025 that it planned to close as many as 150 "low margin" stores over the next three years.

The cuts don't stop there. According to Chain Store Age, Carter's will also eliminate up to 300 office-based positions in the process.

Getty Images

Eddie Bauer

Eddie Bauer is expected to close all of its North American stores after filing for bankruptcy.

Getty Images

Family Dollar

Family Dollar store closings started well before 2026. According to Chain Store Age, the chain shuttered as many as 350 stores between July 7, 2025 and May 12, 2026.

Getty Images

Foot Locker

It's a story of both closings and openings for Foot Locker. ABC News reported in 2025 the chain was planning to close more than 400 stores by the end of 2026.

At the same time, Foot Locker has plans to rebrand some of its locations. Long-time footwear source will retool 280 stores that "focus on its community, power store and play concepts."

Getty Images

Fossil

The Fossil Group has continued to close stores well into 2026. According to The Street, Fossil closed seven locations in the first quarter.

The report stated that the most recent closings bring the total number of shuttered Fossil stores up to 219 in the past five years.

Google Maps image

Fransesca's

Newsweek reported in January that women's clothing stores would be winding down operations. The chain has more than 400 stores in the U.S.

Getty Images

GameStop

In 2024, GameStop closed 590 of its U.S. stores. According to Business Insider. The following year saw a "significant number" of stores close.

Not much has changed in 2026. USA Today reported in January that GameStop had already closed several locations in Ohio, California and Alabama.

Getty Images

Grocery Outlet

At the end of 2025, Grocery Outlet announced plans to close 36 locations. According to The Street, those closures were completed in 2026.

Of the 36 shuttered Grocery Outlet stores, 24 of them were located in the eastern part of the U.S.

Andriy Blokhin // Shutterstock

JCPenney

JCPenney began the first quarter of 2025 by announcing plans to close eight stores. The company had previously filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection. Those stores were shut down for good in May.

In January 2026, the chain closed yet another store in California.

Getty Images

Kroger

Grocery store chain Kroger announced in June 2025 that it had plans to close as many as 60 underperforming locations before the end of 2026. Locations targeted for closure included stores in Illinois, Georgia, Texas and Virginia.

Getty Images

Macy's

Macy's announced in mid-2024 that it would close 150 locations in a span of two years. Initial plans called for closing 50 stores by the end of January 2025. That number was later bumped up to 65 for 2025, with the remainder planned to close in 2026.

Getty Images

Mariano's

Mariano's is a grocery brand owned by Kroger with most of the chains stores located in the suburban Chicago area. According to The Street, the company announced plans to close multiple Mariano's locations by the end of 2026.

Five Chicago-area stores are set for closure by the end of August.

Google Maps image

Mattress Factory

Mattress Factory started 2026 by announcing that it would be permanently closing all of its stores after 26 years in business. The chain's total number of locations had already been whittled down to four coming into 2026.

Getty Images

Publix

Grocery chain Publix is in its second year of slowly closing locations. According to Inc., the chain shuttered 10 stores in 2025. By July 2026, Publix had closed an additional four locations in Florida and Georgia.

Getty Images

REI

REI Co-op announced plans in 2025 to close stores in Paramus, New Jersey, Boston and New York City, with the full plan executed in the first quarter of 2026.

Chain Store Age reported that the closings are part of a strategic plan as the company pushes to "return to growth."

Getty Images

Saks Off 5th and Last Call

Saks Global filed for bankruptcy in January. Following that move, the company will shutter nearly 60 Off 5th locations and another five stores branded with their Last Call concept.

Getty Images

Save A Lot

Discount grocery chain Save A Lot has seen a drastic reduction in store count over the past five to six years. According to Grocery Dive, Save A Lot has lost nearly 400 stores since 2020.

The closings have continued into 2026 as the chain now has around 650 locations.

Getty Images

Torrid

The women's apparel chain informed investors in March 2025 that it would begin its store "optimization" plan and close as many as 50 stores before the end of the year.

Additional closing were later added to the list in 2026.

Google Maps image

Value City Furniture

We didn't even make it two weeks into 2026 before Value City Furniture announced it would begin a liquidation sale at all locations. Expect all stores to close before the end of 2026.

Getty Images

Walgreens

Good Housekeeping reported in 2025 that Walgreens is in the process of closing 2,150 locations by 2027. The total accounts for nearly a quarter of Walgreens' 8,600 stores.

Getty Images

WSS

When Dick's Sporting Goods acquired the Foot Locker brand in late 2025, the company also got its discount shoe chain, WSS. Now, Dick's is looking to lighten the load when it comes to WSS stores.

According to The Street, between the final quarter of 2025 and the first quarter of 2026, more than 50 WSS stores were shuttered, leaving around 100 still in operation.

Getty Images

Yankee Candle

According to Business Insider, Yankee Candle came into 2026 with plans to close as many as 20 locations while also potentially reducing its workforce by 900 people.
